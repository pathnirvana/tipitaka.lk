## Version information

**Promotional text** (170):

The Tipiṭaka in simple, easy Sinhala, with the Pāḷi of the Buddha Jayanthi edition beside it – recitations, dictionaries and search, all offline.

**Description** (4000):

The Sarala Sinhala Tripitaka of pitaka.lk: the suttas in a simple Sinhala translation that everyone can follow, with the Pāḷi text of the Buddha Jayanthi Tipiṭaka. The texts, the search and the dictionaries work offline.

READ
• The Dīgha, Majjhima, Saṃyutta and Aṅguttara Nikāyas, the Dhammapada and more of the Khuddaka Nikāya in simple Sinhala; the parts still being translated show the Pāḷi
• Four ways to read: the Sinhala only, the Pāḷi only, each Pāḷi paragraph followed by its Sinhala, or both side by side on wide screens
• Share a link to a sutta or a paragraph

LISTEN
• Recitations of the Pāḷi texts, with the paragraph being chanted highlighted

LOOK UP
• Tap a Pāḷi word for its meaning: the Buddhadatta and Sumangala Pāḷi–Sinhala dictionaries, the Digital Pāḷi Dictionary (DPD) and Pāḷi–English dictionaries
• The places, people and journeys of the suttas on a map

SEARCH
• Titles and the full text, in Sinhala letters or singlish

Light, sepia, green and dark themes, adjustable text size, bookmarks and a reading history. Free, with no ads, no accounts and no tracking. The recitations need an internet connection.

**Keywords** (100): tripitaka,pitaka,sinhala,pali,buddhism,sutta,dhamma,theravada,sri lanka,dictionary,pirith

**What's new** (4000):

Rebuilt from the ground up: the whole simple Sinhala translation with the Buddha Jayanthi Pāḷi, four reading modes, recitations, dictionaries with the Digital Pāḷi Dictionary, full-text search, the places of the suttas on a map, colour themes and bookmarks.

## App Review notes
No account or sign-in. The texts, search and dictionaries are bundled; the recitations stream from
sajjha.sgp1.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com. This version replaces the earlier app with a new one (the same translation, now
with the Pāḷi text, recitations and dictionaries). Contact: pathnirvana@gmail.com
