## Version information

**Promotional text** (170):

The complete Buddha Jayanthi Tipiṭaka: the Pāḷi texts with their Sinhala translation, the commentaries, recitations and dictionaries – offline.

**Description** (4000):

The complete Buddha Jayanthi Tipiṭaka – the Pāḷi texts with their Sinhala translation – with the commentaries (aṭṭhakathā), recitations, dictionaries and search. The texts, the search and the dictionaries work offline.

READ
• The Vinaya, Sutta and Abhidhamma Piṭaka of the Buddha Jayanthi edition: the Pāḷi text and its Sinhala translation side by side, paragraph by paragraph
• The Pāḷi commentaries (Chaṭṭha Saṅgāyana) and their Sinhala translation, beside the text: tap an underlined word for its explanation, or read both in two columns
• Compare with the scanned pages of the printed books

LISTEN
• Recitations of the Pāḷi texts, with the paragraph and the word being chanted highlighted

LOOK UP
• Tap a word for its meaning: the Buddhadatta and Sumangala Pāḷi–Sinhala dictionaries, the Digital Pāḷi Dictionary (DPD) with grammar, the PTS and other Pāḷi–English dictionaries
• The places, people and journeys of the suttas on a map

SEARCH
• Titles and the full text of the whole canon, in Sinhala letters or singlish, limited to a piṭaka or nikāya if you like

Light, sepia, green and dark themes, adjustable text size, bookmarks and a reading history. Free, with no ads, no accounts and no tracking. The recitations and the scanned pages need an internet connection.

**Keywords** (100): tripitaka,pali,sinhala,buddhism,sutta,dhamma,theravada,atuwa,pirith,sri lanka,dictionary

**What's new** (4000):

Tipitaka 3, rebuilt: the commentary linked to the text, new dictionaries with the Digital Pāḷi Dictionary, the places and people of the suttas on a map, four colour themes and a reading history.
