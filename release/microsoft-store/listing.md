### English (en-us)

**Product name**: Sinhala Tipitaka: Pali & Atuwa

**Description**

The complete Buddha Jayanthi Tipiṭaka – the Pāḷi texts with their Sinhala translation – with the commentaries (aṭṭhakathā), recitations, dictionaries and search. The texts, the search and the dictionaries work offline on your computer.

READ
• The Vinaya, Sutta and Abhidhamma Piṭaka: the Pāḷi text and its Sinhala translation side by side, paragraph by paragraph
• The Pāḷi commentaries and their Sinhala translation, beside the text in two columns
• Compare with the scanned pages of the printed Buddha Jayanthi books

LISTEN
• Recitations of the Pāḷi texts, with the paragraph and the word being chanted highlighted

LOOK UP
• Click a word for its meaning: Pāḷi–Sinhala and Pāḷi–English dictionaries, the Digital Pāḷi Dictionary (DPD) with grammar
• The places, people and journeys of the suttas on a map

SEARCH
• Titles and the full text of the whole canon, in Sinhala letters or singlish

Light, sepia, green and dark themes, adjustable text size, bookmarks and a reading history. Free, with no ads, no accounts and no tracking.

**Short description**: The Buddha Jayanthi Tipitaka – Pāḷi text, Sinhala translation, commentary, recitations, offline.

**Product features**
- The complete Buddha Jayanthi Tipitaka: Pāḷi text and Sinhala translation
- The Pāḷi commentaries and their Sinhala translation, beside the text
- Recitations, with the chanted passage highlighted
- Click a word to look it up in Pāḷi–Sinhala and Pāḷi–English dictionaries
- Scanned pages of the printed books
- Search titles and the full text, in Sinhala or singlish
- Places, people and journeys on a map
