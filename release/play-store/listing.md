### English (en-US)

**App name** (30): Sinhala Tipitaka: Pali & Atuwa

**Short description** (80): The Buddha Jayanthi Tipitaka: Pali text, Sinhala translation, commentary, audio

**Full description** (4000):

The complete Buddha Jayanthi Tipiṭaka – the Pāḷi texts with their Sinhala translation – with the commentaries (aṭṭhakathā), recitations, dictionaries and search. The texts, the search and the dictionaries work offline on your phone.

READ
• The Vinaya, Sutta and Abhidhamma Piṭaka of the Buddha Jayanthi edition: the Pāḷi text and its Sinhala translation side by side, paragraph by paragraph
• The Pāḷi commentaries (Chaṭṭha Saṅgāyana) and their Sinhala translation
• The commentary beside the text: tap an underlined word for its explanation, or read both in two columns
• Compare with the scanned pages of the printed Buddha Jayanthi books
• Open several suttas in tabs or side by side

LISTEN
• Recitations of the Pāḷi texts: the paragraph and the word being chanted are highlighted

LOOK UP
• Tap a word for its meaning: the Buddhadatta and Sumangala Pāḷi–Sinhala dictionaries, the Digital Pāḷi Dictionary (DPD) with grammar, the PTS and other Pāḷi–English dictionaries
• The places, people and journeys of the suttas on a map

SEARCH
• Titles and the full text of the whole canon, in Sinhala letters or singlish
• Limit a search to a piṭaka or nikāya, or to the Pāḷi or Sinhala column

MORE
• Light, sepia, green and dark themes; text size as you like it
• Star suttas and paragraphs; a list of the suttas you read
• Free, with no ads, no accounts and no tracking

The recitations and the scanned pages need an internet connection. Website: https://tipitaka.lk
