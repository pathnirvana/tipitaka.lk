## This book: the Yamaka
The book is made of pairs of questions: a question and its reverse (opened by "යෙ වා පන", "යස්ස වා පන", "යත්ථ වා පන" …).

- In the uddesa sections each sentence is a question, though the Pāḷi has no question word. Word each one as a question ending in "?".
- In the niddesa sections an entry is a question closed by "ති:" and then its answer. Word the question as a question ending in "?", then the answer as plain statements.
- An answer is a bare assent or denial (ආමන්තා, නො, නත්ථි …), or one or more clauses. Each clause names a class of beings, a moment or a place and says which of the two things holds for it: one and not the other ("නො ච …"), or both ("… ච … ච"). Keep each clause as its own sentence, in the Pāḷi's order. State has / has not, and the tense of every verb (present, past, future), exactly as the Pāḷi has them. A denial of a negated verb ("නො ච … න …") stays a double negation: do not turn it into a positive statement, and do not drop either negation.
- Word the same piece the same way every time it comes: the question frame, the opener of the reverse question, each verb in each tense, each class of beings. # TERMS and # PREV show the wording in use.
- Both sides of the source have slips. Where BJT differs from the Pāḷi in a term, a negation or a tense, or repeats or drops words, follow the Pāḷi. Where the Pāḷi itself plainly breaks the pattern of its pair (a term repeated where its partner is required) and BJT has what the pattern requires, follow BJT.
- A label in brackets at the end of a Pāḷi entry (the name of the section just closed) that BJT leaves out: give it in Sinhala, in brackets, at the end.
- Keep every -පෙ- where the Pāḷi has it; do not write out what it stands for.
- A question ends with its verb, then " ද?"; nothing follows the question mark but a space and the answer (no colon). "Both … and …" ("… ච … ච", "… චෙව … ච") is worded with "ද" after each of the two; "one and not the other" opens its second sentence with the wording # TERMS gives for "නො ච".
- A heading or the name of a section is only the name: no explanation in brackets.
